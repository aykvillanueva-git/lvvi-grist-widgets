How these figures are worked out
  • Monthly retainer: expected = 2025 ledger FEE × 12 months; collected = the Jan–Dec 2025 retainer columns. Cash basis: money collected in 2025 counts as 2025, so a December retainer paid in January 2026 shows here as unpaid.
  • Quarterly / annual: expected = quarterly fee × 4 + annual fee (the annual fee collected in 2025 is for the 2024 return); collected = the 2025 quarterly/annual columns. The ledger does not split quarterly from annual, so they are shown together.
  • Balance = expected − collected when positive. Paid over expected = collected − expected when positive; usually 2024 arrears settled in 2025, an advance, or a fee that changed during the year.
  • Fees are the 2025 ledger's FEE column. It can lag a mid-year rate change (Abarcar A shows ₱3,000 while ₱4,500 a month was billed), so confirm a balance against the client's last 2025 SOA before posting it as a 2026 carryover.
  • Check start: the 2025 ledger has no reliable start dates. "Started paying" marks clients whose first 2025 retainer payment came in April or later (possibly new in 2025, so January–March may not be owed); "No 2025 payment" marks a fee with nothing collected all year.
  • Tax & contributions / others are shown per month for reference only; 2025 remittances are not in this file, so no tax or contribution variance is computed.
  • Pimsat is excluded (standing rule). Groups set up in Grist are tagged on the member's row (e.g. Sanchez C → Dela Cruz J); 2025 rows are not combined because the group contract started July 2026.
